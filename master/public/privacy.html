Privacy Policy

Bridge for iOS · Effective 23 October 2026

Bridge is published by For Fox Sake Sanctuary ("we", "us", "our"), a nonprofit organization. This policy explains what the Bridge iOS app collects, why it is collected, and who receives it. It covers the app itself and the servers we operate for it.

1. What the app is

Bridge is a client for a forwarding server. You enter a server address, a Client ID and an Agent Token issued by that server, and the app connects this device to it. The app offers two preset server addresses; both are operated by us. If you enter your own server address, your data goes to that server and is governed by its operator's privacy policy, not this one.

2. Data we collect and why

DataWhy it is needed
Server address, Client ID, Agent TokenTo sign in and to authenticate every request to the server you entered.
A device identifier derived from this installation (for example ios-client-<id>-<16 hex>)To recognise this device, enforce the server's device limit, and list the device in its device manager.
Device model and iOS versionTo select compatible settings and show the device in the server's device list.
Traffic volume (bytes uploaded and downloaded per forward)To account for usage, quotas and limits on the server.
Connection details (line selected, timestamps, error codes)To establish the connection and to troubleshoot it.
Nodes and subscription links you importStored on this device; uploaded to your server only when you add them to its cloud list.
Node addresses and subscription links you enterNode latency tests connect to the node address you entered; a subscription import downloads from the link you entered. These go to those addresses, not to us.

Tunnel logs (connection events, local port numbers, and the hostnames you connect to) are kept on this device and are only shared if you copy or export them.

3. What we never collect

Message or file contents, contacts, photos, location, health data, and the advertising identifier (IDFA). The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK, and does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Who receives it

The data listed above goes to the server address saved in your profile. Your connections are carried through the relay lines that server assigns, so the destinations you connect to pass through those lines. Domain lookups go to the DNS servers set in Tools (default 1.1.1.1; 8.8.8.8 for domains sent direct). We do not sell, rent, use, or disclose this data to third parties for any purpose. We do not share it with data brokers, advertisers, or analytics providers.

To measure line quality, the app sends small connectivity probes to the relay endpoints assigned to your connection and, for the native outbound runtime, a DNS probe to 1.1.1.1:53. These probes carry no personal data beyond the network source address that any internet connection reveals.

5. Retention and deletion

Account and device records are kept while the account is active and for a limited period afterwards for security and accounting, then deleted or anonymised. Deleting a profile in the app removes the profile and its Agent Token from this device; uninstalling the app removes all local data. To delete records held on a server, contact that server's operator — for our servers, use the contact below.

6. Your choices

You can withdraw consent at any time by signing out, deleting the profile, and uninstalling the app. You can ask us to delete the device and account records we hold by writing to the address below. Deleting a profile does not by itself delete records on the server.

7. Security

The Agent Token is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only; it is not copied to backups or to other devices, and it is sent only over HTTPS to the server you entered. Lease material is sealed to a key held by this device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and limit what is collected.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and its effective date. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For Fox Sake Sanctuary · support@forfoxsakesanctuary.org
