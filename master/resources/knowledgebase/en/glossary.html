One short line for each word the other guides use. Open this page whenever a word is unclear, and close it again.

What you will accomplish

You will know what each word in the guides means, without reading anything technical.

Before you begin

  • Nothing to prepare. This page is meant to sit in a second tab.
  • If a word you need is missing, ask support and it gets added.

Steps

  1. Find the word in the table below.
  2. Read the one-line meaning. Stop there if it already makes sense.
  3. Open the guide named next to it when you want to use the word, not just read it.
WordWhat it means
Active planThe plan you paid for that is still valid. Forwards only run while a plan is active.
ForwardOne line you draw on the panel: a destination goes in, a local port comes out.
Forward RouteThe line your traffic leaves from. Pick the one closest to the destination.
Target HostThe address of the game or service you want to reach.
Target PortThe port that address listens on.
Local Listen PortThe port on your own device that the panel hands you. Your game uses this one.
TransportWhether the traffic is TCP, UDP, or both. Games are usually UDP.
ClientThe app that carries the line on your device.
RunningThe state the app shows when the line is up.
Recent syncThe last time the app talked to the panel. A fresh time means the client is healthy.
UltraHow every line is carried. It keeps the route steady while you play.
Application verificationTesting the game itself, not a ping.

How to know it worked

You read a guide and no word stopped you.

If it did not work

Stop and ask support when two guides seem to disagree. Do not guess at settings - a wrong guess costs more time than the question.
