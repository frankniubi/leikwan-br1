A forward is one line you draw on the panel: where your game or service lives, and which local port your device should use to reach it.

What you will accomplish

One saved forward with an assigned local port. The next guide needs that port, so write it down.

Before you begin

  • An active plan, so the panel lets you save a forward.
  • The address and port your game or service really listens on.
  • Which transport it needs: TCP, UDP, or both. Games are usually UDP.

Steps

  1. Open Forwards and click Create Forward.
  2. Pick a Forward Route. Take the line closest to the destination. Every line uses ultra, so there is nothing else to decide.
  3. Fill in Target Host and Target Port. This is the address the game itself connects to, not the address of the site you downloaded it from.
  4. Choose the Transport your service needs: TCP, UDP, or TCP + UDP.
  5. Save. Keep the suggested Local Listen Port unless you have a reason to change it.
  6. Write down the assigned local port. It shows up on the row within a few seconds.

The form only asks for the route, the destination, the transport, and the local port. The rest is filled in for you.

How to know it worked

The row stays saved instead of flipping through provisioning, and the assigned local port is filled in. The Clients page then shows a recent client sync for the device that will carry this forward.

If it did not work

Stop if the panel refuses to save. Three usual reasons: the plan is not active any more, the target host or port is not right, or the local port you typed is already in use - clear it and let the panel suggest one again.
