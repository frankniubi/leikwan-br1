Install the app once on the device you play on, sign in, and leave it running. After that you only open it to check that the line is still up.

What you will accomplish

The app installed on your device, signed in with your panel account, and listed among the online devices on the Clients page.

Before you begin

  • Your panel email and password.
  • The device you play on. The app runs on Windows, macOS, Android, and Linux.
  • Android 8.0+ for phones and tablets.
  • On Linux, Debian 11, 12 or 13 (x86_64 or arm64) and one root command.

Steps

  1. Open the Clients page. Everything you need sits on that one page.
  2. Install the app. On Windows use the download button in the Windows App box. On Android click Download APK. On macOS pick the download for your chip. On Linux copy the command in the Linux box and run it on the target machine.
  3. Sign in with your panel email and password. The app answers Connection successful! when it is in.
  4. Leave it running. The app shows Connected and the switch reads Running.

Every device finishes in the same place: the app is signed in and the Clients page lists it as online.

How to know it worked

Two checks. The app shows Connected, and the online devices list at the bottom of the Clients page shows your device (IP, system and app version).

If it did not work

Stop and sign in again if the app never leaves Connecting.... Most likely first: the password is wrong or the account is not active yet. Then: Android put the app to sleep in the background. Then: the Linux command was run without root.
