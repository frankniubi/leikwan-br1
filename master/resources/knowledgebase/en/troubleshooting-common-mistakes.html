When a game cannot connect, it is almost always one of three things. Check them in this order and you are usually back in a minute or two.

What you will accomplish

Find which of the three usual causes is yours, fix it, and get back into the game.

Before you begin

  • The panel open, and the app running on the device you play on.
  • The guide you followed, in case you need the local port again.

Steps

  1. Is the client online? The online devices list at the bottom of the Clients page should show this device. If it does not, restart the app, wait a few seconds and reload; if the online limit is full, kick a device you no longer use.
  2. Is the address correct? Same device as the client means 127.0.0.1 plus the local port; another device means the client host address.
  3. Is the port allowed? Only the port you actually use has to be open through the firewall, security group, or NAT.
  4. Are the forward and route healthy? In Forward Rules the row should read Running and Endpoint Status should not stay on "No endpoint assigned yet"; the target service should answer when you test it from its own host.
  5. Still stuck? Send support the forward ID, the device you used, and roughly when it failed.

Work down the list once, in order. Skipping a step usually means coming back to it later.

How to know it worked

The app shows the line as running, and Usage this cycle in Forward Rules goes up again while you play.

If it did not work

Stop before changing anything else. Do not change settings at random - it hides the real cause and makes the next round slower. If all four checks are clean, contact support; that is the fastest path from there.
