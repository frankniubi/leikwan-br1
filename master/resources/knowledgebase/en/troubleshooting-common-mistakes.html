When a game cannot connect, it is almost always one of three things. Check them in this order and you are usually back in a minute or two.

What you will accomplish

Find which of the three usual causes is yours, fix it, and get back into the game.

Before you begin

  • The panel open, and the app running on the device you play on.
  • The guide you followed, in case you need the local port again.

Steps

  1. Is the client in sync? The Clients page should show a recent sync. If it does not, restart the app and give it a few seconds.
  2. Is the address correct? Same device as the client means 127.0.0.1 plus the local port; another device means the client host address.
  3. Is the port allowed? Only the port you actually use has to be open through the firewall, security group, or NAT.
  4. Are the forward and route healthy? The row must not sit in provisioning, and the target service should answer when you test it from its own host.
  5. Still stuck? Open View Diagnostics and send the result to support with the name of the forward.

Work down the list once, in order. Skipping a step usually means coming back to it later.

How to know it worked

The app shows the line as running, and the forward row shows traffic again while you play.

If it did not work

Stop before changing anything else. Do not make random topology changes - they hide the real cause and make the next round slower. If all four checks are clean, send support the diagnostics output; that is the fastest path from there.
