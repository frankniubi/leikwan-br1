## What changed
- A stopped forward row now names the cause of its last failed start: local port in use, held by the system, reserved by Windows, or permission required. Before, it showed a generic "Unavailable".
- The proxy banner names the forward it belongs to ("Forward #N: ...").
- Ultra start failures keep the local cause on the inner exception, so the card can read it.
