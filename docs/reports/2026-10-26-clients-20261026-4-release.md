## What changed
- Dashboard: the plan card is gone (the panel sends no plan name). Usage, rate limit and home province remain above the forward list.
  - Home province has explicit states: 未连接, 读取中, 读取失败, 未设置, or the province name. Disconnecting clears the previous account's value.
- Forward cards show the target address and port. A target that cannot be parsed is shown whole, with no port.
- Native outbound rules on the dashboard show a pointer to the sidebar 原生出口 page instead of their 不可用 state and message.
- Connect page: one scroll area with a sticky section bar at the top (转发线路 / 服务与节点). The bar follows the scroll position and jumps to each section.
  - Fix: the 转发线路 column sat in a nested ScrollViewer, which swallowed the mouse wheel even with nothing to scroll. The nested ScrollViewer is removed.
- Sidebar: 服务与节点 is no longer its own item; it lives on the 连接 page. Old view ids (`Nodes`, `Forwards`) still open the connect page.
