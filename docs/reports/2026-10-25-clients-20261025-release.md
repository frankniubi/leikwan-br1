## What changed
- IPv6 always captured by the tunnel (Android, Windows TUN, iOS, OpenWrt); no silent fallback to another
  line or region; node/forward changes while connected apply or are locked with a reason; reconnect after
  crash, sleep or network change (Windows); VPN rebuilt after Android kills it, with a battery prompt;
  iOS On Demand and stale-lease stop.
- macOS is Developer ID signed and notarized (app and DMG, stapled).
- Update gate (inert): Windows, macOS, Android, iOS show a non-dismissible prompt when the panel sends
  `client_update`. Linux and OpenWrt never receive that field.

## Runtime and attestation
- Windows: all 13 bundled helpers are byte-identical to 0.1.20.68 (ultra-client `ddb8b98e…`). No rollover.
- Android: tracked runtime unchanged (arm64 `018170e8…`, x86_64 `563e7d29…`, as registered). APK signer
  certificates are identical to the published 0.2.44 (v3 lineage, `700adc0a…` / `2fde6e1e…`).
- macOS: helpers were rebuilt in CI, so every helper differs from 1.0.58. The runtime now stamps
  fingerprint `2026.10.24.1.0ab3e18a`; its signed hash `4578a440…5a6f` was added to
  `ULTRA_ATTESTATION_EXTRA_HASHES_JSON` before the DMG was published. Old 1.0.58 hashes stay accepted.
